    The rulebooks UAE IT teams work to.

    My day-to-day practices — least-privilege access, firewall policy, patching, backup, monitoring and documentation — are the building blocks these frameworks ask for.

    Federal law

    UAE PDPL

    Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021

    Governs how personal data is collected and protected. Access control, secure VPNs and reliable backup support it directly.

    Dubai Government

    DESC ISR

    Dubai Electronic Security Centre

    The Information Security Regulation for Dubai Government entities — policy-driven firewalling, patching and monitoring are core controls.

    International

    ISO/IEC 27001

    Information security management

    The widely used standard across UAE enterprises: documented procedures, asset management and continual improvement.

    Free zones

    DIFC & ADGM

    Own data-protection regimes

    Financial free zones run their own rules alongside federal law — I adapt network and access policy to whichever rulebook applies.

    This maps where everyday engineering practice supports each framework. It is not a compliance certification or audit credential.
